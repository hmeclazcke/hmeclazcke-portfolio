# Product Vision

## Purpose

hmeclazcke-portfolio is Hernán Meclazcke's completed static technical portfolio. It goes beyond a traditional résumé or LinkedIn profile by presenting technical history and evidence in an exploratory, owner-curated format.

## Product Identity

The portfolio is not an online CV clone and does not replace LinkedIn. LinkedIn remains the destination for detailed professional experience. The site focuses on:

- a concise technical introduction;
- a personal career and learning Story;
- technologies represented through truthful historical contexts;
- an evidence-oriented Technology Graph; and
- minimal links to approved public profiles.

## Completed Experience

The final public website contains four major destinations:

### Home

A concise introduction to Hernán's technical identity with entry points to the Story and Technology Graph.

### Explore My Story

A responsive career and learning chronology that provides personal context without duplicating a traditional employment history.

### Technology Graph

An interactive browser-side graph of technologies learned or used across professional, learning, and portfolio contexts. It derives its relationships from project-owned structured data. Contexts are evidence, not visible graph nodes, and graph edges describe shared context rather than technical dependency.

### Contact Footer

A minimal final destination with LinkedIn and GitHub icon links over a decorative Piedra Movediza illustration.

## Product Principles

- Present a portfolio, not a résumé clone.
- Keep technology evidence truthful, contextual, and independent from UI rendering.
- Distinguish current and historical experience without implying unsupported proficiency or professional claims.
- Keep the site understandable to non-technical visitors while providing useful depth for technical reviewers.
- Preserve the dark, restrained visual identity and accessible responsive behavior.
- Keep the complete core product statically deployable through GitHub Pages.
- Add technology or infrastructure only when it serves a real product need.

## Scope Closure

The static portfolio is the finished product scope. The repository documentation records its engineering process and historical specifications; that information is not an additional website feature.

There is no committed next phase or future capability. Any future product decision requires a new owner-approved specification.

## Non-Goals

The completed product does not:

- replace LinkedIn or duplicate a full employment history;
- provide a downloadable CV by default;
- add email, phone, or a contact form;
- depend on a backend, runtime GitHub data, or external APIs;
- introduce technologies solely to enlarge the implementation stack; or
- become more complex for visual or architectural effect.
